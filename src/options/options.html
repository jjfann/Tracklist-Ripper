Tracklist Ripper

Dig the tracklist out of a YouTube mix and press it into a Spotify playlist.

1 · Connect Spotify

Spotify requires every tool like this to run through its own developer app. You make one for yourself, and it's free and takes a couple of minutes. Spotify only lets development apps work when the app owner has Premium.

2 · On-device AI optional

If your Chrome has Gemini Nano, it reads the messy lines, like ones with no dash or with the title before the artist. It runs entirely on your computer and is free. Without it, the built-in parser does the job.

4 · Demo mode

Try everything without a Spotify connection. The YouTube buttons, tracklist reading and on-device AI are all real, but a pretend Spotify "finds" whatever was read, and nothing is sent anywhere. It's handy for checking how well a tracklist parses. Real matching quality needs the real Spotify.

Your Spotify tokens stay in this browser. Tracklists are only sent to Spotify's search.
